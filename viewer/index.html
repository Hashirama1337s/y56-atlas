A straight line can lie inside a curved surface, the way straight steel beams make up a curved cooling tower. Y56 is a smooth surface cut out by a single equation of degree 4, and it holds 56 perfectly straight lines, all of them real: the most real lines any smooth real surface of degree 4 can hold, and Y56 is the only one that does it (Degtyarev, Itenberg and Sertöz proved this; with complex lines allowed, the record is 64). Spin it, touch a line to read its exact coordinates (whole numbers and √2), turn the family dial to watch 16 lines flash into existence at one single setting, and see the same equation through six different meanings of “real”.

How to read the ball. All of space, including its points at infinity (projective space), is drawn as this glass ball. Whatever leaves through the glass comes back in at the exactly opposite point, so a straight line shows up as an arc running from one side of the ball to the opposite side.

The equation and the picture

F = 3√2 z0²z1z2 + 3√2 z1z2z3² − √2 z1³z2 − √2 z1z2³ + 4z0³z3 − 4z0z3³

F = Im(w4) + Im(p2)(3|w|2 − |p|2)/√2,  w = z0 + i z3, p = z1 + i z2

The picture uses coordinates y with z1 = 21/8y1 and z2 = 21/8y2, in which Y56 is the member s = 3/21/4 (s4 = 81/2) of the family Gs. Exact coordinates on the cards are in the original coordinates z. The ball is centred on the point (0 : 1 : 1 : 0); 16 of the 32 symmetries fix it, and they show up as the four-fold turn about the vertical axis, the mirrors and the point reflection that you can see in the picture.

Every curve is computed from the exact data (coordinates over Q(√2)); each drawn sample point satisfies the equation to about one part in ten million. The surface is drawn by marching cubes in your browser, so its edges are accurate to the grid.

Y56 and the theorem that 56 real lines is the maximum, reached only by Y56, are due to Degtyarev, Itenberg and Sertöz (arXiv:1601.04238). The count of 636 conics is due to Degtyarev (arXiv:2407.00493). The exact line list, the conics drawn here, the family dial and the six-faces table: Moki & Julio (AI-assisted).
